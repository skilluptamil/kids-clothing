Order Processing

  • Orders placed before 2pm on business days ship the same day.
  • Orders placed on weekends or holidays ship the next business day.
  • You'll receive a confirmation email and a tracking link when your order leaves our studio.

Delivery Times

Delivery estimates are calculated from dispatch, not order placement, and are not guaranteed. During peak seasons (holidays and sales) there may be slight delays.

International Shipping

We ship to over 40 countries. Import duties and taxes may apply and are the responsibility of the recipient unless stated otherwise at checkout.

Missing or Damaged Orders

If an order arrives damaged or doesn't arrive, contact us within 7 days of delivery (or within 30 days for missing packages) and we'll make it right — replacement or refund.
