Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help websites work properly and remember your preferences. Here's how we use them.

Cookies We Use

TypeWhat It DoesExamples
Strictly NecessaryKeep the site secure and functional.Cart contents, security tokens

Managing Cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. Blocking all cookies may affect parts of the site, such as keeping items in your cart.

Local Storage

In addition to cookies, we use browser local storage to remember your theme choice, reading direction and cart between visits. This data stays on your device.

Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are set by trusted partners (such as analytics and payment providers) on our behalf. We don't control these third-party cookies.
