30-Day Returns

Unworn items with original tags can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund or exchange — no questions asked.

What's Eligible

  • Unworn, unwashed items in original condition with tags attached.
  • Exchanges are free on standard shipping.
  • Final sale items cannot be returned unless faulty.

What's Not Eligible

  • Items worn, washed or with removed tags.
  • Undergarments and items in contact with skin (for hygiene reasons).
  • Gift cards and final-sale clearance items.

Refund Timeline

Once your return is scanned by our warehouse, refunds are processed within 3–5 business days to your original payment method. You'll get an email at every step.

Faulty or Wrong Items

If something arrives faulty or isn't what you ordered, tell us within 7 days and we'll arrange a free replacement or full refund, including return shipping.
