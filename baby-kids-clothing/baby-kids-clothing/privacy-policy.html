LittleBloom ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it and the choices you have. This is a template policy for demonstration purposes.

1. Information We Collect

When you use our site, we may collect:

  • Contact details you provide (name, email, phone, delivery address).
  • Order and payment information (processed securely by our payment providers).
  • Device and usage data such as browser type and pages visited.
  • Preferences you express, like saved sizes or wishlist items.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and deliver your orders.
  • To send order updates and service messages.
  • To personalise your shopping experience.
  • To send marketing, only if you opt in.
  • To improve our website and prevent fraud.

3. Cookies & Similar Technologies

We use cookies to keep your cart, remember preferences and understand how our site is used. See our Cookie Policy for full details.

4. Sharing Your Information

We never sell your personal data. We share information only with trusted service providers (shipping partners, payment processors) who are bound by confidentiality, and when required by law.

5. Data Retention & Security

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above, then delete or anonymise it. We use industry-standard safeguards including encryption in transit.

6. Your Rights

You may access, correct or delete your personal information, object to processing, and withdraw consent at any time. Contact us and we'll respond within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

Our site is intended for parents and guardians. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Contact Us

Questions about this policy? Email privacy@littlebloom.shop or visit our contact page.
