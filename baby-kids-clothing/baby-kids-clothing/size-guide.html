Measurement Guide

How to Measure Your Child

Take accurate measurements in 3 quick steps before choosing a size.

1
Height

Stand your little one against a flat wall, mark the top of their head, and measure barefoot from floor to mark.

2
Chest & Waist

Measure around the fullest part of the chest and natural waistline, keeping tape level and comfortably relaxed.

3
Weight

Weigh without nappies or shoes. When in between sizes, we always recommend sizing up for longer wear.

Pro Tips for a Perfect Fit
  • Measure a freshly washed, worn item your child already fits to compare dimensions accurately.
  • Kids grow quickly — choose a little extra room for seasonal coats & jackets.
  • Pyjamas and sleepwear should fit snugly and securely for safety.
