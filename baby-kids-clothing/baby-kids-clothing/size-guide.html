1. Height

Stand your little one against a wall, mark the top of the head and measure from floor to mark.

2. Chest

Measure around the fullest part of the chest, keeping the tape comfortably loose.

3. Weight

Weigh without nappies or shoes. When between sizes, we recommend sizing up.

Pro Tips for a Perfect Fit
  • Measure a freshly washed, worn item your child already fits to compare sizes.
  • Kids grow fast — plan for a little extra room in outerwear.
  • Pyjamas and sleepwear should fit snugly for safety.
  • Not sure? Chat with us — we love helping with fits.
